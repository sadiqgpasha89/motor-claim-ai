2B. Risk-Based Claim Processing & Dynamic Re-Tiering (IN-RISK-PROCESSING-2026)

Not every motor claim belongs in the same workflow. Deterministic, explainable risk classification routes claims into 3 operational paths, recalculates whenever new evidence arrives (so a claim starting as LOW RISK can escalate to HIGH RISK later), automatically preserves evidence on high risk, and guarantees that fraud_signal_score itself NEVER determines claim rejection.

LOW RISK · Fast-Track path: fast_track

Fast-Track Claims

Handling: Straight-through assisted processing
  • Minor damage (non-EV HV, non-injury, drivable)
  • Complete documents attached & verified
  • Valid active policy & incident within period
  • Low estimate (< ₹50,000 IRDAI gate)
  • No material anomalies or open alerts
  • Automated processing & auto-approval limit ≤ ₹25,000 (L1 assisted up to ₹49,999.99)
Goal: Minimize human touchpoints & bypass physical survey delay.
MEDIUM RISK · Standard path: standard

Standard Claims

Handling: AI-assisted claims handling
  • Damage requires inspection (₹50k+, recapture, teardown hidden damage)
  • Estimate discrepancies (>12% variance, duplicates, supplement quotes)
  • Missing documents follow-up checklist
  • Coverage clarification & deductible verification
  • Repair coordination (IIISLA surveyor, garage, towing)
Goal: Reduce handler effort and waiting time with AI assistance & Maker-Checker DoA.
HIGH RISK · Complex / SIU path: complex_investigation

Complex / Investigation Claims

Handling: Specialist and human-led handling
  • Injury or third-party liability (including multiple vehicles involved)
  • Suspected fraud (advisory score escalates to SIU — never auto-rejects)
  • Significant loss (≥ ₹2,50,000, CTL ≥ 75% IDV, fire/flood/theft, airbags)
  • Conflicting evidence (RC/Policy mismatch, prompt injection)
  • EV battery damage (traction battery / HV isolation impact)
  • Legal or coverage disputes (inactive policy, open dispute grievance)
Goal: Improve investigative quality, preserve evidence (legal_hold=true), and ensure controlled decisions.

3. Bounded Action-Taking, Rework Loops & Decision Intelligence

Upgrading our 11 bounded agents in-place without duplicating agents — adding strict dependency gating, rework loops, safe tool-driven execution, and complete decision explainability.

A. Agent-to-Agent Orchestration

Dependency-Gated DAG & Recovery

  • Structured Publications: Every agent publishes typed published_result payloads onto the workflow ledger.
  • Strict Dependency Gating: Downstream stages return 409 BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY until upstream prerequisites succeed.
  • Rework Protocol: Coverage, Estimate Validation, and QA Compliance emit typed ReworkRequests back to earlier stages and invalidate stale downstream outputs.
  • Retries, Timeouts & Recovery: Auto-retries transient timeouts, creates critical escalation tasks, and resumes from checkpoint via /orchestration/recover.
B. Action-Taking Agents

Safe Execution via Controlled Tools

  • create_claim_task & request_documents: Persists prioritized tasks and sends missing-doc/recapture checklists.
  • schedule_inspection, notify_garage & dispatch_towing: Books lowest-load verified surveyors, honours garage preferences (with EV safety override), and dispatches flatbed towing.
  • update_claim_status: Transitions states strictly through validate_transition().
  • generate_approval_request & initiate_approved_payment: Generates Maker-Checker DoA tasks and releases payouts only after Finance + Checker sign-off.
C. Decision Intelligence

Transparent Why-Path Ledger

  • Evidence & Citations: Links exact extracted facts, SHA-256 hashes, rate-card benchmarks, and policy clauses.
  • Reason Codes & Confidence: Machine-readable codes (RC_REPAIR_VS_REPLACE_REWORK, RC_EV_GARAGE_SAFETY_OVERRIDE) + calibrated scores.
  • Routing Outcome & Alternatives: Shows chosen route alongside rejected/considered alternatives.

4. What Insurers, Hiring Managers & Architects Need to Know

Direct operational answers to the eight questions every technical evaluator or insurance executive asks when inspecting an agentic claims platform.

Autonomous Execution Lane (Zero Manual Effort)

How a Claim Moves Forward Without a Human Doing Every Step

  • Idempotent FNOL Registration: Duplicate retries replay safely; missing documents generate checklists without stalling intake.
  • Automated Evidence Quarantine: Magic-byte MIME verification, EICAR malware checks, SHA-256 hashing, and prompt-injection stripping run automatically on upload.
  • Policy Clause RAG & Triage: Hybrid TF-IDF + keyword search retrieves exact UIN clauses while deterministic IRDAI ₹50k rules assign queues and SLA timers.
  • Vision + Rate-Card Checking: 6-angle image completeness, visible damage tagging, and OEM rate-card variance checks run automatically on garage quotes.
  • Decimal Settlement Prep & QA: Line-item depreciation, Zero-Dep waivers, and pre-closure payment/grievance checks compute deterministically.
Human-in-the-Loop Authority Lane (High-Judgment Only)

What the Human Claims Officer Actually Needs to Review

  • Document & Security Exceptions: Verifying mismatched RC plates (CLM-0003), quarantined prompt injections, or requesting daylight photo recaptures (CLM-0004).
  • Estimate Discrepancies & Supplements: Accepting, amending, or rejecting flagged duplicate lines, >12% rate variances (CLM-0001), or hidden teardown damage (CLM-0007).
  • SIU Fraud Disposition: Inspecting explainable indicators + benign explanations and marking substantiated vs not_substantiated (CLM-0008).
  • Independent Maker-Checker DoA: Approving payouts within role limits (L1 ₹75k, L2 ₹5L, Legal TP ₹15L) — same-user approval blocked with 403.
  • Finance Release & Timeout Recon: Releasing idempotent payouts and reconciling timed-out gateway instructions (CLM-0009).

What Happens When an Agent or Dependency Fails? (Resilience & Safety Matrix)

LLM / Vision Model Outage ModelGateway raises ModelOutageError (circuit open) BoundedAgent.execute() catches error and returns status="needs_human", confidence=0.0, fallback_mode="manual_workflow". FNOL, Decimal math & DoA approvals keep running normally.
Agent Calls Unauthorized Tool BoundedAgent.call_tool() allowlist check Raises ToolPermissionError immediately; agents cannot read raw DB tables outside scope or trigger payments.
Prompt Injection in Uploaded PDF sanitize_untrusted_text() regex filter Replaces attack directive with [FILTERED_UNTRUSTED_DIRECTIVE], sets prompt_injection_flagged=true, forces DocumentIntelligence to NEEDS_HUMAN.
Payment Gateway Timeout IntegrationAdapterRegistry.execute_payment() Persists timeout_reconcile_pending (PG-PEND-*). Re-submitting same Idempotency-Key replays existing record cleanly; Finance resolves via POST /reconcile.
Self-Approval or Bulk Approval validate_approval_authority() & /approvals/bulk Returns 403 NOT_AUTHORISED immediately; enforces independent Checker and individual claim review.
